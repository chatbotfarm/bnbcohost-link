Quick answers

Common questions

Do you manage short-term and mid-term rentals? +

Yes — we support both STR and mid-term stays depending on the property and operating needs. Mention your rental type on your discovery call and we'll tailor the scope.

Do you mark up housekeeping or vendor quotes? +

No — day-to-day housekeeping, routine repairs, and annual maintenance are passed through at cost with owner approval. You see the vendor's direct quote before we proceed.

What about major renovation projects? +

For renovations over $5,000, we charge a 15% management fee covering scope, coordination, scheduling, and quality assurance. You get a clean packaged process, not a mystery invoice.

Do you work outside Maryland / DC / Virginia? +

Yes — we are increasingly remote-capable. Book a discovery call and we'll discuss what coverage looks like for your specific property and market.

How quickly can you get started? +

Onboarding timeline depends on property location and service scope. We'll give you a realistic start date on your discovery call — typically within 1–2 weeks for housekeeping and turnovers.
